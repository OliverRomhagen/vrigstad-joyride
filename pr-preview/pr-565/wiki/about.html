  • The real Vrigstad

    Every building, road, field and lake comes from OpenStreetMap and real elevation data. Drive past the church, round Slättsjön, up Gästgivaregatan.

    Up to three stars, one police car each. Lose them for 18 seconds and they go back to their coffee.

    Three houses around the village. Get inside with no police within 60 m, lie low for a minute, and the stars are gone.

    A car legally limited to 30 km/h, a Swedish rural classic. Perfect for getaways, if nobody is chasing you.
