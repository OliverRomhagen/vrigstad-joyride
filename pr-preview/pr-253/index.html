Vrigstad Joyride

The whole village at street level: 881 buildings and every street from the real map. Walk around, borrow a Volvo 240 without asking, and try not to get caught.

Move / drive
W A S D or arrow keys. On a phone, the left stick.
Steal / get out
E (or the Use button)
Sprint / jump
Shift and Space on foot. In a car, Space brakes.
Drift
Space and steer at speed. Hold the slide until the sparks turn blue or orange, then let go for a boost
Look around
Move the mouse (Esc frees the cursor). On a phone, drag the view.
Lie low
Go inside a safehouse (green house on the minimap) for a minute
