Vrigstad

The Vrigstad wiki

The places and people of Vrigstad Joyride, a driving game set in a real village in Småland, where nothing ever happens until you take a car.

Places

  • Kiosken The kiosk on Torget. Korv me brö, cat treats and trisslotter.
  • Slättsjön The lake by the village. Cars don't float.
  • Torget The village square, where every run starts and every arrest ends.
  • Vrigstadsån The river through the village, from the north-east past Torget and out west to Lundholmssjön.

People

  • Plåt-Pelle Runs Skroten, the scrapyard on Stockarydsvägen. Buys anything that rolls, and most things that don't.

Wildlife

  • Cats Vrigstad's cats sit by the houses and watch the traffic. Some of them like you.

From the game

  • The map Vrigstad in 3D, built from the same data the game drives on.
  • The garage Every car in the game on Plåt-Pelle's ring, with the numbers it drives by.
Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Vrigstad is a real village in Småland; the game and this wiki are made with affection for it.
