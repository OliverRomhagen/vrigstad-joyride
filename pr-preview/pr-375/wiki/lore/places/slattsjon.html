Slättsjön

The lake by the village. Cars don't float.

#water

Drive here x -433 m, z -835 m in the game's coordinates

Open full screen ↗

Slättsjön is the lake next to Vrigstad. In summer people swim from the jetty; in the game, people mostly try to jump it.

They don't make it. Cars sink.
