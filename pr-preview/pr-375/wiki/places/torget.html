Torget

The village square, where every run starts and every arrest ends.

Torget is the square in the middle of Vrigstad, and the origin of the game's map (x = 0, z = 0) is a few metres away. You start here, and when the police catch you (Gripen!), you're back here.

The kiosk is on the square. It sells snacks and trisslotter, and it's the safest place in the village to lose your temper.

From Torget, Gästgivaregatan leads out of the village; Slättsjön is a short drive away.
