Place

Vrigstadsån

The river through the village, from the north-east past Torget and out west to Lundholmssjön.

Vrigstadsån comes in from the north-east, winds through Vrigstad close by Torget, and runs out west into Lundholmssjön.

In the game it runs in its own channel, cut down below the fields and the streets, with stylised water and shores along its banks. Where a road or a path crosses it there is a bridge, built from the map's data: Jönköpingsvägen crosses on a road bridge right by Torget, with walkways on both sides, and footbridges carry the paths. A car fits between a road bridge's railings, but not onto a footbridge.

If you'd rather see how deep it is, Slättsjön has more water.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Vrigstad is a real village in Småland; the game and this wiki are made with affection for it.
