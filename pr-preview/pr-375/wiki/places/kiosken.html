Kiosken

The kiosk on Torget. Korv me brö, cat treats and trisslotter.

Kiosken stands on Torget. Walk up to the counter and press E to see what it has. You start the game with a little money in your pocket, and this is where most of it goes.

A korv me brö or a smålandsrulle puts some health back after a crash. The treats are for the cats.

The trisslott

A trisslott is a scratch card. Most of them win nothing, and losing a few in a row doesn't do much for your mood. What happens when you win the big one, you'll have to find out.
