Vrigstadsån comes in from the north-east, winds through Vrigstad close by [Torget](torget.md), and runs out west into Lundholmssjön.

In the game it runs in its own channel, cut down below the fields and the streets, with stylised water and shores along its banks. Where a road or a path crosses it there is a bridge, built from the map's data: Jönköpingsvägen crosses on a road bridge right by Torget, with walkways on both sides, and footbridges carry the paths. A car fits between a road bridge's railings, but not onto a footbridge.

If you'd rather see how deep it is, [Slättsjön](slattsjon.md) has more water.
