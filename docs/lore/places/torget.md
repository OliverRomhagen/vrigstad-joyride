Torget is the square in the middle of Vrigstad, and the origin of the game's map (x = 0, z = 0) is a few metres away. You start here, and when the police catch you (_Gripen!_), you're back here.

[The kiosk](kiosken.md) is on the square. It sells snacks and trisslotter, and it's the safest place in the village to lose your temper.

From Torget, Gästgivaregatan leads out of the village; [Slättsjön](slattsjon.md) is a short drive away.
