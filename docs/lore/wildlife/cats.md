Cats live all over Vrigstad. You'll find them sitting along the walls of the houses, out of the wind, keeping an eye on whoever walks past. Each one has its own coat: ginger, black, grey, white, brown, or black with a white bib.

Walk up to one and press E to pet it. Not every cat is in the mood, and a cat that has had enough lets you know. They don't like cars, or people running at them. Be patient, and one of them may decide that it's yours.

A treat helps; [the kiosk](../places/kiosken.md) sells them.

Keep your eyes open while you drive. Cats get about more than you'd think.
