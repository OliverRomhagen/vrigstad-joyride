Plåt-Pelle runs Skroten, the scrapyard on Stockarydsvägen. Blue overalls, a red cap with a peak, a grey beard, and a habit of turning to watch you as you walk into his yard.

Drive a car onto the ring inside the gate and he'll make you an offer: up to 1 600 kr for a clean one, scrap value for a wreck. He won't touch a police car, and he won't deal with you while you have stars.

His yard is also [the garage](https://oliverromhagen.github.io/vrigstad-joyride/wiki/garage), where every car in the game stands on the ring for you to look at.
